Steps to take right now

1

Stop all contact immediately

Do not respond to the scammer. Block their number/email. Do not send any more money, even if threatened.

2

Secure your accounts

Change passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and alert your bank or card issuer to dispute any charges.

3

Document everything

Screenshot messages, note dates, amounts, and names. Save any receipts or transaction IDs — these are vital for reports.

4

File an official report

Report to the relevant authority below. You can file to multiple agencies at once — more reports increase investigation priority.

Official reporting agencies

FTC — ReportFraud.ftc.gov FBI IC3 — ic3.gov International — econsumer.gov FTC scam alerts Identity theft — IdentityTheft.gov USA.gov fraud guide
